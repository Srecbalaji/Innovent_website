Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Innovent Creations ("we", "us", or "our") handles information submitted through this website (innoventcreations.in).

Information We Collect

When you use the project enquiry form on this website, you may provide:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your company or brand name (optional)
  • Details about your creative project
  • Budget and timeline preferences (optional)

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your project enquiry and to communicate with you about your creative requirements. We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your consent.

Data Storage

Enquiry information is processed and stored in accordance with the form provider we use. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies

This website does not currently use tracking cookies. If this changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at innoventcreations@gmail.com.
